# 🧵 Dhaaga v0.1.0 Prototype

> **Connecting Hands to Markets — From Village Craft to Global Cart in Under 5 Minutes.**

First working prototype release of **Dhaaga (धागा • ShilpSetu)** for Android.

---

### ✨ What's New

* **🗣️ Pre-Warmed Voice Guide:** Instant audio onboarding in **Hindi & English** with zero cold-start delay.
* **🌐 22 Indian Languages:** Real-time localized interface with regional craft vocabularies.
* **📸 AI Image Studio:** Automatic background removal cutout (ML Kit) and framing guides.
* **🧵 5-Step Craft Listing:** Voice memos, auto-categorization, fair-price guidance, and instant publish.
* **🏷️ Discounts & Coupons:** Direct sale pricing, automated coupon codes, time limits (10m–3mo), and usage caps.
* **🔍 Voice Search:** Search crafts hands-free using speech recognition on the home marketplace.
* **🛍️ Storytelling & Cart:** Cultural narratives ("Kahaani"), coupon redemption at checkout, and simulated UPI escrow.
* **☁️ Cloud & Stock Sync:** Bidirectional Firestore sync with permanent local persistence and auto-restocking on cancellation.
